Privacy Policy

Buffalo Retro Player Lite · Last updated: 5 November 2026

Buffalo Intelligent Studio ("we") makes Buffalo Retro Player Lite (the "App"), a music player for the music files on your own device. This policy explains what data the App uses.

Your music and settings

The App reads the music files you allow it to access and keeps its library, playlists, play counts, ratings and settings on your device only. We do not receive them. We have no account system and no servers of our own that collect your data.

Internet features you choose to use

  • Podcasts: when you search for or subscribe to podcasts, the App contacts the Apple iTunes Search API and the podcast's own server to fetch the feed, artwork and episodes.
  • Find Missing Artwork: sends album and artist names to MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive to find cover art.

These services have their own privacy policies.

Advertising

The free App shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your approximate location (from your IP address),
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy Options in the App. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ads in the App are muted and never interrupt your music.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
